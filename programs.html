Overview

Hands-on upskilling covering AI & Agents, Robotics, Embedded Systems and IoT. Build real projects at your pace.

School students (Class 8+) and college students.

No prior coding experience required. A basic laptop is recommended.

None. Curiosity and willingness to build.

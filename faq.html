FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before enrolling.

Programs

What programs are currently available?

One program is currently open: the 4-Week AI, Robotics & IoT Cohort for school and college students. More tracks are planned.

What will I learn?

AI & Agents, Robotics, Embedded Systems and IoT — 4 modules, 8 sessions, 4 hands-on projects.

Is prior coding experience required?

No. The program is designed for beginners. No prior coding experience needed.

Eligibility

Who can join?

School students (Class 8+) and college students. Other audiences are planned for future tracks.

Do I need a laptop?

Yes, a basic laptop is recommended. Hardware kits are provided during sessions.

Learning format

How are sessions conducted?

Live, mentor-led sessions in small batches, in your town.

How long is the program?

Modular and paced to you — 4 modules, 2 sessions per module plus 1 project each. Complete at your own pace.

Hardware

Do I need to buy hardware?

No. Hardware kits are provided during sessions. No upfront purchase required.

Fees & certificates

What is the fee?

₹999 per student for the full program, all four tracks included.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of completion after the capstone demo day.

Enrollment

Are seats limited?

Yes, limited seats per batch to ensure mentor attention.

Career support

Do you guarantee jobs or placements?

No. We do not make job or placement guarantees. We focus on building real skills and projects that strengthen your profile.

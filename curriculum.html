Curriculum

Deep, modular curriculum.

Each track is broken into 8 modules. Go deep on one track or combine them. Paced to you — not a fixed schedule.

Track 1

AI & Agents — 8 modules

1

AI Foundations

What AI is, how LLMs work, tokens, context, capabilities and limits.

2

Prompt Engineering

Prompt patterns, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role and constraints, iteration.

3

AI Tools & Workflows

Claude, ChatGPT, tool use, file handling, productivity workflows.

4

Agents & Tool Use

Agent design, tool calling, memory, planning, evaluation.

5

Agentic Patterns

Multi-step, multi-agent, reflection, retrieval, orchestration.

6

Automation with AI

Automating real tasks, integrations, triggers, scheduling.

7

Safety & Evaluation

Hallucinations, bias, testing, guardrails, responsible use.

8

Capstone: AI Assistant

Build and ship a working personal AI assistant.

Track 2

Robotics — 8 modules

1

Electronics Basics

Voltage, current, resistance, components, safety.

2

Microcontrollers

Arduino/ESP32 setup, IDE, flashing, serial monitor.

3

GPIO & Sensors

Digital/analog I/O, reading sensors, debouncing.

4

Motors & Actuators

DC motors, servos, motor drivers, PWM, H-bridge.

5

Control Logic

Line following, obstacle avoidance, state machines.

6

Communication

Serial, I2C, SPI, UART between modules.

7

Integration

Combining sensors, motors and logic into a robot.

8

Capstone: Working Robot

Build and demo a complete robot.

1

C/C++ for Embedded

2

Microcontroller Architecture

Registers, clocks, peripherals, memory map.

3

GPIO Deep Dive

Pin modes, pull-ups, interrupts, debouncing.

4

Timers & PWM

Timers, PWM generation, frequency control.

5

Communication Protocols

I2C, SPI, UART in depth.

6

Sensors & ADC

Analog reading, calibration, filtering.

7

Power & Optimization

Low power, sleep modes, efficient code.

8

Capstone: Sensor Node

Build a reliable embedded sensor node.

IoT Concepts

What IoT is, architecture, use cases, protocols overview.

2

WiFi & Bluetooth

3

MQTT

Pub/sub, topics, QoS, brokers, clients.

4

Cloud Platforms

IoT clouds, dashboards, data storage.

5

Sensors to Cloud

End-to-end data pipeline from device to dashboard.

6

Remote Control

Actuation from cloud, commands, feedback loops.

7

Security Basics

Auth, TLS, secure credentials, device identity.

8

Capstone: Smart Device

Build a smart device with a cloud dashboard.
